# Privacy Policy for ShellZip

**Last updated:** 8 November 2026  
**Publisher:** Menonsoftware  
**Product:** ShellZip (Microsoft Store and related desktop builds)

## Overview

ShellZip is a desktop archive manager. It creates, opens, lists, tests, and extracts archive files on your device. This policy describes what information ShellZip handles and what it does not.

## Information we do not collect

ShellZip does **not**:

- Require an account with Menonsoftware  
- Include advertising, trackers, or analytics SDKs  
- Sell or rent personal data  
- Upload your archives, passwords, or file contents to Menonsoftware servers  

Menonsoftware does not operate a backend that receives usage telemetry from ShellZip.

## Information processed on your device

ShellZip processes data **locally** on your computer, including:

- Archive files and folders you open, create, or extract  
- Paths and names of files you choose  
- Optional archive passwords you enter for encryption or decryption  
- Application preferences (for example theme, compression defaults, recent paths)

On Windows Store installs, preferences are stored under your Windows local app data for ShellZip (outside the install folder). Passwords you type for archives are **not** saved in the preferences file.

You control that data by deleting archives, clearing recent history in the app (where available), or uninstalling ShellZip (which removes the Store package; leftover local settings folders may remain until you delete them).

## Network use

**Microsoft Store install:** Updates are delivered by the Microsoft Store. Microsoft’s own privacy policy applies to Store services, account data, and download telemetry Microsoft may collect.

**Optional update check (non-Store / Velopack builds):** If you install ShellZip outside the Store and use “Check for updates,” the app may contact **GitHub** to see whether a newer release exists. That request can expose standard network metadata (such as IP address and time) to GitHub under GitHub’s privacy policy. ShellZip does not send your archives or passwords in that check.

ShellZip does not otherwise require network access to pack or unpack files.

## File associations and shell integration

If you allow ShellZip to open archive types, Windows may launch ShellZip when you open those files. That stays on your device. Cascaded Explorer context-menu integration used by non-Store installs is separate from the Store package’s manifest associations.

## Children

ShellZip is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Third parties

Depending on how you obtained ShellZip, third parties involved may include:

- **Microsoft** — Store distribution, updates, and related services  
- **GitHub** — hosting of source code and, for some non-Store builds, release packages / update feeds  

Those companies process data under their own policies when you use their services.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the product or practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
